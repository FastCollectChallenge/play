How to play

Return to the game

1. The goal

Move your mouse to guide the ball and catch the apples that appear. The rarer an apple is, the more it is worth.

2. The two modes

  • Arcade mode: you have 60 seconds to score as many points as possible. A red apple is worth 1 point and a green apple 3 points.
  • Adventure mode: no time limit. Collected apples go to your inventory and are recorded in the Index. You can sell them, buy potions, trade with other players, and fuse apples.

3. Selling your apples

  1. Open the Inventory with the button on the side of the screen.
  2. Press Sell on an apple, then choose the quantity with the -1, -3, -5, -10, None, +1, +3, +5, +10 and All buttons.
  3. With Select apples to sell you can pick several at once, or select them all.
  4. Use Autosell to automatically sell the apples you choose as soon as you collect them.

5. The shop and luck potions

In the Shop you can buy potions that multiply your luck of finding rare apples, from x2 to x30, for 5 to 60 seconds. Prices range from $1,000 to $150,000. You can also resell a potion from your inventory and get 80% of its price back.

6. Trading with other players

  1. Open Trade and search for a player by username.
  2. Send a trade request. The other player must be online and accept it.
  3. Each of you adds the apples you want to give to your offer.
  4. When your offer is final, press Ready. When you are both ready, press Confirm.
  5. The trade completes after a short countdown.

7. The Witch Fuse

Enter the witch's fuse machine, place 4 apples inside, then press Fuse. You may get a special apple, or nothing at all. The recipes are secret: it is up to you to discover them.

8. Your account

  • Log in or sign up with a username and password, with Google, or with GitHub. Your progress is then saved online.
  • You can also play as a guest. Your progress then stays on this device, and you can import it into an account later.
  • In the account settings you can change your profile picture. Use the Upload button to host an image, then paste its address.

9. Live events

While you play, messages at the top of the screen announce events for all players: global luck, Green Time (more green apples), Double Points, or special apples spawning on your server.

10. Tips

  • Activate a luck potion before hunting for rare apples.
  • Put common apples on autosell so you do not waste time.
  • Log in to an account so you never lose your progress.
